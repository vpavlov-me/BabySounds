Privacy Policy

BabySounds is designed to keep bedtime playback simple and private. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information stored on your device

BabySounds may store app preferences locally on your device, including onboarding state, favorites, playback settings, timer settings, and widget playback state. This information is used to make the app work.

Purchases and subscriptions

Premium subscriptions are processed by Apple through StoreKit. BabySounds receives subscription status from Apple so it can unlock premium features. Payment details are handled by Apple and are not available to BabySounds.

Feedback and support

If you contact support or send feedback through TestFlight, your message may include information you choose to provide, such as screenshots, device details, app version, and a description of the issue.

Diagnostics

Apple may provide crash reports, diagnostics, and TestFlight feedback through App Store Connect. This information is used to fix bugs and improve app reliability.

Microphone

The current app is focused on playback. If a future version adds optional audio calibration, microphone access will only be requested when needed and only after iOS shows a permission prompt.

Children

BabySounds is intended to be used by parents or caregivers. The app is not intended to collect personal information from children.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support information on the Support page.
