TestFlight Guide

BabySounds beta builds are installed through Apple's TestFlight app. This is the normal over-the-air path for testing on a personal iPhone before App Store release.

Install on iPhone

  1. Install TestFlight from the App Store.
  2. Open the invitation email or beta link from your iPhone.
  3. Accept the invitation in TestFlight.
  4. Tap Install next to BabySounds.
  5. After installation, open BabySounds from TestFlight or the Home Screen.

What to test

  • First launch and onboarding.
  • Starting, pausing, and switching sounds.
  • Sleep timer and fade-out behavior.
  • Background playback after locking the phone.
  • Lock Screen and Control Center playback controls.
  • Favorites and widget quick-start actions.
  • Premium paywall, purchase, and Restore Purchases in sandbox testing.

Send feedback

Open TestFlight, select BabySounds, and tap Send Beta Feedback. Include what happened, what you expected, and whether the issue repeats.
